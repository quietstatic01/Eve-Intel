Local Intel

Version 2.2

A live picture of the space around you in EVE Online: kills, traffic, ratting, intel, sov and wormholes, with alerts. It runs entirely in your browser from this one file.

Getting started

  1. Open the page in Chrome or Edge on the computer that runs EVE.
  2. Type a system name at the top right, choose how many jumps around it to cover (1 to 5) and press Load. Or connect your chat logs folder (see Intel and location) and the page starts in the system you're in.
  3. Open Alerts and settings, below the map, to choose what alerts you and how.

A link like index.html?system=NAME&jumps=3 always opens that system. Otherwise the page starts where you are, or in the last system you loaded.

Watched systems

  • Type system names into the box at the top, separated by commas, and press Watch. They can be anywhere, on the map or not.
  • Each card shows kills in the last hour and 24 hours, ship jumps and NPC kills in the last hour, the latest intel that mentions it, how far it is from you, and tags for gate camps, big ships, sov, ESS and wormholes.
  • Center map moves the map to that system. Remove stops watching it.
  • With "Always alert on … watched systems" on, any intel, new kill, gate camp or sov fight there alerts you, however far away it is.
  • Kills in watched systems also appear in the kill feed, marked "watched".

EVE login and notifications

Log in with EVE to see your characters' notifications in the Character notifications panel: structures under attack, shields and armor lost with the timer, fuel and low power warnings, sov events, wars, kill reports and the rest. Attacks put an ATTACKED tag on the system for an hour.

One-time setup:

  1. Put the page on a web address. EVE's login can't return to a file opened from your disk. Two ways:
    • GitHub Pages: make a repository, upload this file as index.html, then in the repository's Settings → Pages choose to deploy from the main branch. The page appears at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/REPOSITORY/.
    • Your own computer: in the folder with the file, run python -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000/.
  2. Go to developers.eveonline.com, sign in, and create an application. Choose the type that allows authentication and API access, add the permission esi-characters.read_notifications.v1, and set the callback URL to the address shown under EVE login in Alerts and settings (the Copy button copies it).
  3. Copy the app's Client ID into the Client ID box. The page doesn't need the secret key.
  4. Press Log in with EVE and approve. Repeat for each character you want.

Notifications update every 5 minutes, but CCP only refreshes them about every 10, so they can be up to 10 minutes behind. Notifications that were already there when you log in don't alert. Your login is saved in this browser only. Log out removes it and cancels it with CCP.

Gate map

  • Systems sit in rings by jumps from the center. Lines are stargates.
  • Dot color: red is a kill in the last hour, orange a kill in the last 6 hours, blue is ship traffic only, grey is quiet. Bigger dots mean more ship jumps in the last hour.
  • Green glow is ratting: NPC kills in the last hour. The label shows rats per hour.
  • A purple ring is a system named in intel in the last 15 minutes. It pulses for the first 3 minutes. A number in brackets is the reported pilot count.
  • A dashed ring and YOU mark where your character is.
  • Labels: CAMP possible gate camp, big ship type (e.g. DICTOR), VULN sov hub vulnerable, FIGHT sov fight, ESS ESS activity reported, ATTACKED a structure attack from your notifications, TH/TU Thera or Turnur wormhole.
  • Click a system to show only its kills in the kill feed. Click it again to show all.

Intel and location

  • EVE writes each chat channel to a file on your computer. In EVE's settings, turn on Log Chat to File.
  • Type your intel channel's name exactly as it appears on its tab (several, separated by commas), then click Connect chat logs folder and pick Documents\EVE\logs\Chatlogs. Next time, press Reconnect folder.
  • The page reads new messages every 1.5 seconds. It recognises system names, and shortened names (the first few characters) when only one system matches.
  • Pilot counts: "+5", "5x", "5 nv", "5 reds", "local 12" and "x5". "clr" or "clear" sets the count to 0. A count only applies when a message names one system.
  • Pilot names in intel are looked up: corp, alliance, zKillboard danger and solo %, kills, usual ships. "CLOAKY HUNTER?" and "CYNO ALT?" are guesses from their history.
  • Big-ship words (titan, super, dread, carrier, fax, lancer, rorq, dictor, hic, blops, caps, cyno) and ship names like "Sabre" get tagged.
  • ESS words (ess, linking, main bank, reserve bank, bank) get an ESS tag.
  • Show lists all intel, or only messages about systems on the map or watched.
  • Your location comes from your Local chat log, updated within seconds of each jump. With several characters logged in, each one is shown.

Kill feed

  • The last 24 hours, newest first, up to 150 kills, with ship, system, gate, victim, attackers and value. Each kill links to zKillboard.
  • Tags: LOSS a friendly died, KILL friendlies got the kill, HOSTILE DOWN a listed hostile died, STRUCTURE a structure died, and big ship types.

Alerts and settings

  • Alert on intel within N jumps, counted from where you are or from the map's center.
  • Alert as a sound, a desktop pop-up, spoken words, or any mix. The browser tab also flashes when the page is in the background. Test alert shows what you'll get.
  • Optional alerts: new kills and gate camps in range; big ships anywhere on the map; sov fights and structure kills anywhere on the map, and ESS reports in range; anything in watched systems.
  • Follow me re-centers the map each time you jump.
  • Switches for ratting glow, sov and ESS, and Thera and Turnur wormholes.
  • Standings: full alliance or corp names (not tickers) for friendly and hostile. You can hide friendly losses from the kill feed.

Settings are saved in your browser.

How often it updates

Intel and your locationevery 1.5 seconds
Kill feed and kill countsevery 2 minutes
Hourly stats, sov, wormholesevery 10 minutes
New log files (after relogging)every 30 seconds
Character notificationsevery 5 minutes (CCP refreshes about every 10)

Refresh at the top updates kills, hourly stats and notifications right away.

What it can't show

  • Local member counts, anomalies and signatures, ESS bank values, other people's structure timers and jump bridges. None of these are published by CCP. Your own structure timers appear only when a notification includes them.
  • Chrome may block desktop pop-ups for a file opened from your disk. Hosting the page on a website fixes that.

Data: CCP's EVE Swagger Interface and EVE login, zKillboard and EVE-Scout.
